Customer care

Return, exchange & refund policy

Eligible standard pieces: 7 days from delivery. Please request approval before sending anything back.

View my orders

At KsquareGems, every piece is carefully inspected before shipping. Many of our products are handmade, customized, or made specifically for each customer. Please review the following policy before placing your order.

Returns

We accept eligible return requests within 7 days of delivery.

To qualify for a return:

  • The item must be unused, unworn, undamaged, and in its original condition.
  • All original packaging, tags, accessories, and included items must be returned.
  • You must contact us before sending any item back.
  • Items returned without prior approval may not be accepted.

After receiving the returned item, we will inspect it before approving the refund.

Non-returnable items

Custom orders and personalized name or letter pendants cannot be returned. All other regular products, including watches and grills, may be requested for return within 7 days of delivery, subject to the condition requirements above and admin approval.

If a custom or personalized item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact our team with photos so we can review the issue.

Exchanges

Eligible non-custom products may be exchanged within 7 days of delivery, subject to availability.

If you would like a different size, model, style, or product, please contact us first.

Any price difference between the original product and the replacement product must be paid by the customer.

Watches

Eligible standard watch models may be returned within 7 days of delivery if:

  • The watch has not been worn.
  • Protective stickers have not been removed.
  • The watch has not been resized, scratched, damaged, or modified.
  • The watch is returned with all original packaging and accessories.

Customized watches, custom dials, custom stone settings, engraved watches, or specially requested modifications are final sale.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

Please provide:

  • Your order number
  • Clear photos of the item
  • Clear photos/video showing the issue
  • Photos of the packaging if the parcel was damaged during shipping

After reviewing the issue, we may offer a repair, replacement, exchange, or refund, depending on the situation.

Return Shipping

For returns due to a change of mind, incorrect size selected by the customer, or other customer-related reasons, return shipping costs are the customer’s responsibility.

The customer is responsible for safely returning the item until it is received by KsquareGems.

We strongly recommend using a tracked and insured shipping service.

If the return is required because of an error made by KsquareGems, we will work with you to provide an appropriate solution.

Customs Duties & Taxes

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, VAT, brokerage charges, or other fees charged by their country.

Customs duties and import charges are non-refundable.

If a parcel is refused because of customs charges or import requirements, any shipping, customs, handling, or return-related costs may be deducted from the refund where permitted.

Refunds

After you submit a request, our team will review it and approve or decline it. We will contact you through WhatsApp or email to discuss the return, explain shipping charges and any other applicable deductions, and agree the refund amount with you. The agreed amount and our decision note will appear in your order. Saving an agreed amount does not mean payment has been issued. Approved refunds are processed after the returned item has been received and inspected.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method whenever possible.

Original shipping charges, express shipping fees, customs duties, payment processing charges, and other non-refundable costs may be excluded from the refund where applicable.

Please allow your bank or payment provider additional processing time after the refund has been issued.

Order Cancellation

For standard products, please contact us as quickly as possible if you need to cancel your order.

For custom or made-to-order products, cancellation may not be possible once any of the following has started:

  • CAD/design work
  • Material preparation
  • Stone setting
  • Production
  • Engraving
  • Custom sizing

Design or CAD charges already incurred may be non-refundable.

Repairs & After-Sales Support

We want our customers to enjoy their jewelry for a long time.

If your jewelry needs repair, stone replacement, polishing, rhodium plating, resizing, or another service, please contact us with photos/videos of the item.

Repair and shipping charges will depend on the condition of the product and whether the issue is covered by our after-sales support.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Before returning any product, please contact KsquareGems with:

  • Your name
  • Order number
  • Reason for return/exchange
  • Photos or video if there is an issue with the product

Please do not ship a return until you have received return instructions from us.

KsquareGems reserves the right to refuse returns that do not meet the conditions stated above.

This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights that may apply under applicable law.
